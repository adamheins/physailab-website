Physical AI Lab

Vision

Our goal is to accelerate the pace of scientific discovery using physical AI that understands the physical world and reasons about science, from how a robot should physically interact with a material to how that interaction affects the experiment and its scientific outcome.

To that end, we are building the ChatGPT of the physical lab: a general-purpose system for lab automation that scientists can instruct naturally and that reliably executes and adapts to new experimental workflows rather than being reprogrammed for every task.
